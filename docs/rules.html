Community rules

Every user must accept these before they can post anything.

Treat the birds well

No cruelty, no fighting, and no content showing a bird being harmed. Only domestic pigeons may be listed. Wild, protected or endangered species are not allowed under any circumstances.

Be honest in your ads

Describe the bird as it actually is: breed, age and condition. No fake or borrowed photos, no pretending to be someone else, and never ask a buyer for an advance payment or a one-time password.

Keep it respectful

No abuse, threats, harassment or hateful content. No nudity or sexual content. Anyone can block another user, and blocking hides all content in both directions.

Report anything that breaks these rules

Every ad, reel, post, comment, message and profile carries a Report option. Reports are reviewed by a moderator. Content reported by enough separate accounts is hidden automatically while it waits for review.

Enforcement

Breaking these rules can result in content being removed and the account being suspended. Serious cases, especially those involving cruelty or protected species, are removed permanently.
